========================================================================
   AuraMarket Multi-Vendor Demo: How to Upload to Your cPanel / Hosting
========================================================================

Aap is folder ('ecommerce-demo') ko apne cPanel hosting par upload karke
apne domain par instant live CodeCanyon-style demo chala sakte hain!

STEPS (1 Minute Ka Kaam):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Apne cPanel me Login karein aur "File Manager" open karein.
2. 'public_html' folder ke andar jayein.
3. Ek naya folder banayein jiska naam rakhein: 'ecommerce-demo' 
   (ya jo bhi naam aap URL me chahte hain, jaise 'demo').
4. Is 'ecommerce-demo' folder ke andar jo files hain:
   - index.html
   - assets/ (poora folder)
   inhe wahan upload kar dein!
5. Ho gaya! Ab aapka live demo link ready hai:
   👉 https://aapkiwebsite.com/ecommerce-demo/

DEMO CONTROLS AVAILABLE IN THIS LINK:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Desktop Mode: Full-screen responsive web view.
- iPhone / Mobile Bezel Simulator: Screen ke beech me real phone mockup
  chalega jisme client scroll karke mobile app jaisa feel le sakega.
- Tablet (iPad) Mode: Tablet screen simulator.
- 1-Click Panel Switcher:
  * Customer Storefront (Products, Variants, Split Cart, Checkout, OTP Stepper)
  * Super Admin Panel (Bento Analytics, 1-Click Wipe/Seed, Theme Manager)
  * Vendor Portal (Inventory, SKU creation, Sub-order dispatch stepper)
  * Courier Hub (Pickup/Drop maps, 4-digit OTP handover)
- 1-Click Reset: Ek click me fresh showroom data reset.
- Hide Bar Button: CodeCanyon bar ko hide karke direct standalone view.

Sabhi cheezein 100% production-tested hain!
